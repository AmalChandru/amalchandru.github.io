2024 was a busy year. I shifted two houses this year, visited four
places on my travel list, completed two half marathons, and went on a
long bike trip of 650+ kilometers. I also found myself in some tough
conversations and situations.

Here are some lessons I learned this year. Some are common sense, but
I’ve included them because I tend to overlook the obvious. Some are my
own realizations, and others come from wise people I’ve met along the
way:

- **Uncomfortable conversations** happen more often than expected;
  developing the skill to handle them is a plus.
- **Investing** is important, but regularly reviewing and rebalancing
  your portfolio is just as crucial.
- **Taxes** matter learning about them is helpful, especially in India.
- Being a **generalist** or a **specialist** significantly influences
  your opportunities and job satisfaction.
- For long-distance biking, always **research the route**, **check
  weather**, **fuel stops**, and know basic **bike maintenance**.
- Focus on **form** while running; it helps prevent injuries and boosts
  performance.
- Understand how your work fits into your company’s **goals**.
- Sometimes **boring technology** works the best.
- Bob’s Bar in Indiranagar, Bangalore serves amazing dosa.
- **Know who you’re voting for** and understand what they stand for.
